North Atlantic Ocean, near the Azores

The Mystery of the Mary Celeste

DISCOVERED: DECEMBER 4, 1872

On December 4, 1872, the merchant brigantine Mary Celeste was found adrift and abandoned in the Atlantic Ocean near the Azores, sailing without a soul aboard. Her cargo was undisturbed, the crew's belongings were still in place, and the single lifeboat was missing—yet Captain Benjamin Briggs, his family, and the entire seven-man crew were never seen again.

West Pittston, Pennsylvania

The Smurl Poltergeist

DATES: 1974 – 1989

Starting in 1974 and escalating through the 1980s, the Smurl family of West Pittston, Pennsylvania reported years of disturbing phenomena in their duplex home—foul odors, disembodied voices, physical assaults, and apparitions. The case drew in paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren, sparked national media coverage, and remains one of the most publicized alleged haunting cases in American history.

Lincoln, Massachusetts

The Disappearance of Joan Risch

DATE: OCTOBER 24, 1961

On October 24, 1961, Joan Risch vanished from her home in Lincoln, Massachusetts, leaving behind a blood-smeared kitchen, an unfinished sandwich, and a trail of blood leading out to the street—then nothing. Despite a massive search of the surrounding woods and swamps, no trace of Joan was ever found, leaving one of New England's most enduring missing-persons mysteries unresolved.

About Midnight at Wolfcloud Station

WORLDWIDE MYSTERIES BROADCAST FROM OUR SECLUDED LOOKOUT TOWER

Midnight at Wolfcloud Station is an independent true crime docuseries and podcast investigating cold cases, strange disappearances, and unsolved mysteries from across the globe.

Hosted by Sierra Sterling, the channel uses a cinematic, slow-burn 1970s desaturated graphic novel aesthetic. In the lore of the channel, our team broadcasts from a remote lookout station high in the misty Pacific Northwest wilderness.
